The Tales of Highlander

Minimum System Requirements
Requires 64 Bit CPU and OS
OS: Windows 7, 8.1, 10, and 11. Linux 6.0+
CPU: Intel Core i3 2100, equivalent or higher
GPU: Intel HD Graphics 2000, equivalent or higher
RAM: 1GB
Storage: 1GB Free
DirectX: 9.0+
OpenGL: 2.0+
Additional Notes: mac support will be available in the future.

Recommended System Requirements
Requires 64 Bit CPU and OS
OS: Windows 10, and 11. Linux 7.0+
CPU: Intel Core i3 4130, equivalent or higher
GPU: Intel HD Graphics 4400, equivalent or higher
RAM: 2GB
Storage: 1GB Free
DirectX: 11.0+
OpenGL: 2.0+
Additional Notes: mac support will be available in the future.
